    Questions, answered.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    How does the free plan work?
    You pick one family (Macro, QuantFi, Premia or Edge) and use it free, forever, with no usage limit and no card. You sign in with Discord. Pro (€{price}/month) unlocks all four families, also unlimited.
    
    
    What does Pro include, and how do I pay?
    Pro is €{price} a month for unlimited use of all four families ({total} tools). Billing runs through Whop. Subscribe from the pricing section; the free plan stays open to everyone.
    
    
    Can I see the code?
    No. Rabbit is a hosted, closed-source service: free to use, but the server code isn't published. The repository behind this website contains only the static site.
    
    
    Do results show where the data comes from?
    Yes. Premia and official-data results always show their source, with attribution to the publishing agency, for example BLS, SEC or BEA. Rabbit is independent and not endorsed by those agencies.
    
    
    Is this investment advice?
    No. Rabbit provides research tooling. Backtests are simulations, not forecasts, and data may be delayed or incomplete. Nothing here promises returns.
    
    
    What about the Discord bot?
    The Edge paper-ranking bot is free for everyone inside the official Discord server, The Rabbit Hole. Join with the invite link and run /papers.
    
    
    Does the endpoint URL change?
    It may. The current endpoint is shown in the connect section. If Rabbit moves to its own domain, this page will show the new URL and the old one will keep working during a transition period.
    
    
    Do you see my code or touch my broker?
    No. Calculations and charts are assembled in your own assistant. Rabbit's tools are read-only research tools; they don't run your code or connect to your brokerage.
